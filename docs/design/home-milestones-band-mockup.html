The decisions this wireframe makes, and why

  • THE ORDER IS THE PROTOCOL’S OWN CHAIN, and it answers a visitor’s questions in the order they ask them. Top row — people join (Membership) → money routes through (Economy) → supply is destroyed (Fire): that is literally how the mechanism works, learned left to right without a paragraph. Bottom row — the market has depth (Liquidity) → members bring members (Referral) → the record accumulates (Archive). At three columns the pairs stack meaningfully too: Membership over Liquidity (people, then the market), Economy over Referral (money in, money shared), Fire over Archive (what is burned, what is kept). Deliberately NOT ordered by progress — that would re-sort itself every time a figure moved and would read as arranged rather than true.
  • “Milestone” is the thing, “sealed” is what happens to it. A first-time reader meets a word they know (milestone) and learns the house word from context (2 sealed · nine sealed so far). “Seal” is not accidental jargon — it carries what no synonym does: it is permanent and does not replay. It stays, and the plain sentence underneath teaches it in one read.
  • The chapter lead is gone. It said “14 of 333” — which the hero and the era band already say. Cutting it removes the fourth framing of one number at the source instead of wording around it. Each card owns a “Next milestone”, which is a target, not a rival denominator.
  • No serif heading. The hero owns the serif voice on this page; a second display line competed with it. The eyebrow now IS the heading — quiet, and still a real <h2> so the section is not an unlabelled region.
  • Shape matched to the treasury cards above: 12px radius, 24px padding, round icon discs — the same shapes as the AVAX / BTC / ETH / USDC cards, so the two bands read as one family instead of two components.
  • New Economy icon. The old one was three linked nodes — nearly the referral icon. Economy is now money entering and being SPLIT onward: one line in, three arrows out. Referral keeps the network language; Economy owns flow. No glyph in this set promises a gain.
  • Figures are today’s live values (14 seats · 210.00 USDC routed · 31,272 SYN burned · 17 artifacts · 3 sources · 4 additions · 9 sealed). In the build they come from the milestone read-model — never typed.

Type note: this wireframe requests Instrument Serif / Work Sans / IBM Plex Mono exactly as the site does, but a preview cannot load external fonts — some text may fall back. The built page uses the real faces; geometry, sizes and spacing are the binding part.
