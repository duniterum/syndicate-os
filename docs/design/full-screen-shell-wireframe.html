What this wireframe locks (before any code)

  • One width contract for header + body + footer — the footer stops being narrower than the header.
  • App / data pages (Proof, Activity, Wallet, Referral…) fill the width; columns multiply, no empty margins.
  • Long-text pages (Whitepaper, Terms, Privacy, Risk) keep a readable column centered in the full frame.
  • Mobile handled to AAA at every breakpoint 320→2560: stacked columns, footer accordion, ≥44px targets, safe-areas.
